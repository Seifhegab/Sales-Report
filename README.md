# Sales-Report

It is PowerBI dashboard that shows insights of sales of 2009 and 2008 and is there a growth or not and what products are sold and the type of customers and what did they but during the two years

As we see in dashboard Total Market Overview:
1. Total Sales for 2009: $29M, achieving 74% of the 2009 forecast, with a growth rate of -5.5% compared to the previous year.
2. Year-over-Year Variance: A decrease of $1.7M, broken down as follows:
   - China: Increase of approximately $3M.
   - USA: Decrease of around $2M.
   - Germany: Decrease of approximately $3M.
3. Variance vs. Forecast: A shortfall of $9.97M, distributed as follows:
   - USA: Contributed to the largest variance with approximately -$7.90M.
   - China and Germany: Each decreased by around -$1M.
4. Top Brand Performance: The brand with the best performance against the forecast is Southbridge Video at 82%, followed closely by Proseware at 81%.
5. Category, Performance: Cell phones led the category with 60% of sales, although they did not meet forecast expectations.
6. Highest Product Sales: The top-selling product is the Contoso Projector 1080p X980 in white, generating around $264K in 2009 (compared to $154K in 2008).
7. Top Customers: The second highest customer by 2009 sales is Adams, Andrea, with sales of around $5.2K (the first customer is a placeholder name, which we will discuss during our call).
